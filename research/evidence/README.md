# Research evidence handling

Evidence was collected for the Folioyard product study on 2026-10-12. `screenshots/manifest.json` maps each first-screen capture to its REA Evidence ID, SHA-256 digest, and source URL. Screenshots are internal comparative research; do not reuse competitor logos, copy, illustrations, or layouts in Folioyard.

`REA_COMPETITOR_ENTRY_PAGES.json` and `REA_PDFGEAR_MERGE.json` are retained raw REA outputs. They include captured DOM/accessibility material and navigation/network metadata. Keep these files private to the project research workspace; do not publish or ship them with the application. The written research uses short paraphrases and points to the public source pages.
